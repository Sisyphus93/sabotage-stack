      How to play

      1. 1Watch the block. It slides across the top of your tower. Drop with Space, a tap on the canvas, or the Drop button. Solo auto-drop is 3.5 seconds by default; choose 10 seconds or turn it off in the title settings. The timer pauses for the first 2 seconds of each vote. 10-second and off runs are labelled Assisted.
      2. 2Keep the overlap. The part that hangs over falls away. A small tick marks the centre of the top block. Line up the centres within 3 pixels for a perfect drop and keep your full width.
      3. 3Survive chat. The first vote opens after 8 seconds, then every 10 seconds (7 seconds from height 25). The block keeps moving during Solo and Competitive votes; Teamwork votes happen between turns, when teammates can vote and the host breaks ties. In Teamwork, a timed-out turn is skipped without dropping; idle players can return with I’m ready to play, and the host can skip a turn. In Solo and Competitive, most sabotages last 5 seconds; Crumble, Portal Walls, Superglue, and Calipers affect up to 3 drops or 8 seconds. In Teamwork, sabotages last until the affected landing, or 3 affected landings for those four effects, with no time cap. A forfeited turn does not count; later votes queue until the active sabotage ends.
      4. 4Try again. A miss ends the run. Your best height stays for this session only.

      Playing together

      The host opens a Teamwork or Competitive room and shares its code. The host watches and controls the room rather than placing blocks; 2–6 players join from separate browser tabs or devices. At least two connected players are needed to start.

      Teamwork: Players take turns on one shared tower. Your team score is the height plus the number of perfect drops. Players vote between turns; the host breaks tied votes.

      Competitive: Each player builds a separate tower. After 90 seconds, the highest tower wins; equal heights tie. A player who misses finishes their own stack. The host has one changeable vote for each sabotage.
